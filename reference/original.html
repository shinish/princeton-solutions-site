Approach

How an engagement runs

A familiar audit discipline, applied with enough flexibility to fit your calendar and your teams.

  1. Scope

    Agree objectives, criteria, frameworks and stakeholders. Confirm what evidence will satisfy each requirement.

  2. Assess

    Walk through processes, test controls and validate evidence with control owners.

  3. Report

    Deliver risk-rated findings, root causes and practical recommendations in plain language.

  4. Validate

    Track corrective actions and independently confirm remediation through closure.
